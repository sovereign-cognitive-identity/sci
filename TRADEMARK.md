## What the AGPL-3.0 license does and does not cover

The AGPL-3.0 license grants you broad rights to use, modify, and distribute the Sci *software*. It does not grant any rights to use the Sci *name or logo* as trademarks.

Trademark law is separate from copyright/software licensing. You can run Sci software under AGPL-3.0 without a trademark license. You cannot represent that your product *is* Sci or is *endorsed by* Sci without permission.

## Permitted uses (no permission needed)
