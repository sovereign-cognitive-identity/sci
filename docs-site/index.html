Sovereign Cognitive Identity

The privacy layer between you and every AI

Sci anonymizes everything you send to an LLM, de-anonymizes everything it sends back, and keeps the only copy of who-you-are on hardware you control. This site documents what exists today and where the project is headed.

Two tracks, one project. The Product track explains what Sci is and why it matters. The Architecture track is for developers and contributors — how each subsystem works and how to extend it. The Roadmap shows the six-phase evolution from today's alpha to the full vision.
