## Why a CLA?

Sci is dual-licensed under AGPL-3.0 (open source) and a Commercial License (for managed service operators). The CLA gives the Maintainer the right to include your contributions in both licensed versions. Without this, your contributions could only be used under AGPL-3.0, which would prevent the managed tier from including community contributions.

This is the same model used by MongoDB, Elastic, and other dual-licensed OSS projects.
